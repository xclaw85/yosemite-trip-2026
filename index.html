⚠ Heat advisory for your exact dates

The National Weather Service forecasts high risk of extreme heat in the Central Valley August 1–2 and moderate extreme-heat risk in the Sierra Nevada August 1–4. Central Valley highs of 105–113°F. This is the hottest stretch California has seen in about two years, and it lands on your arrival and first two full days.

Plan around it: hike at dawn, gain elevation midday, be in the river by 1 PM. Re-check weather.gov the night before you leave.

The plan in one picture

Climb out of the heat

Valley floor sits at 4,000 ft and will be near 100°F. Every 1,000 ft of gain buys roughly 3 to 5 degrees. Tuesday is the escape day.

Do this first

Three things to verify

Any one of these can derail the trip. Sort them before you pack.

01

Is Curry Village actually booked?

August is peak season. Call (888) 413-8869 and confirm you have a confirmation number, not a browsing session. Backups: Housekeeping Camp, Yosemite Valley Lodge, or outside the park in El Portal, Midpines, Mariposa, or Oakhurst.

02

Confirm the bed configuration

Tent cabins come as 1 double, or 1 double + 1 single, or 1 double + 3 singles. Roll-away beds and cribs are not available. Three people in a two-bed unit means someone shares a double. Make sure the third person is on the reservation.

03

The Mist Trail is closed on your weekdays

Trail repairs run July 27 through October. The section above the Vernal Fall footbridge is closed Monday through Thursday, 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM. There is a workaround, see below.

Deposit is 100% of the first night at booking, and cancellations need 7 or more days notice. At this point, treat the booking as non-refundable.

What the heat changes

  • The unheated canvas tent will be miserable from roughly 1 PM to 6 PM. Be in the river or at the pool.
  • Nights in the low 60s are mild, but still bring a sleeping bag. Provided bedding is thin.
  • Check coolant, radiator, and tire pressure before you leave. The CA-58 Tehachapi grade and the Hwy 41 climb are where cars overheat at 110°F.
  • Carry a gallon of water for the car on top of your drinking water.
  • Consider leaving Lake Arrowhead at 5:30 AM instead of 6:30 to clear Bakersfield and Fresno before the worst.

Closure

Getting to Vernal Fall anyway

Set expectations now: there is very little actual mist in August. The falls run on late-season snowmelt, not spring runoff. Still spectacular, just not the drenching-spray version in the photos.

Day by day

Itinerary

Built around one rule: move early, rest hot, move again late.

Day 1

Sunday, August 2

Drive in

CA-18 to Victorville, I-15 N, US-395 N, CA-58 W to Bakersfield, CA-99 N to Fresno, then CA-41 N through Oakhurst to the South Entrance. About 350 miles, 6.5 hours of driving, 8 with stops.

  • 6:30 AMDepart Lake Arrowhead. Make it 5:30 if you want to beat the heat.
  • 8:00 AMVictorville / Kramer Junction, quick stop
  • 9:45 AMBakersfield, gas and stretch
  • 11:30 AMFresno: lunch and supermarket run. Last real grocery store.
  • 1:15 PMOakhurst: final gas fill
  • 1:45 PMSouth Entrance. Budget 30 to 90 minutes in line.
  • 3:00 PMTunnel View
  • 3:30 PMArrive Curry Village. Park once and leave the car.
  • 4:00 PMCheck in. Long line. Send one person to wait while the others unload.
  • 4:45 PMMove in. Everything scented into the bear locker.
  • 6:00 PMDinner at Pizza Deck or Seven Tents Pavilion
  • 7:30 PMWalk to Stoneman Meadow for alpenglow on Half Dome

No entrance reservation is required in 2026, but pre-buy the $35 pass on Recreation.gov to speed up the gate. Card only, no cash. Cell service dies around Oakhurst, so download offline maps first.

Day 2

Monday, August 3

Vernal Fall at dawn
  • 5:45 AMLeave Curry on foot, or catch the first shuttle to stop #16
  • 6:00 AMUp the John Muir Trail to Clark Point, then the cutoff to the top of Vernal Fall. Carry 2 liters each.
  • 11:00 AMBack at camp
  • 1:00 PMSwinging Bridge on the Merced, or the Curry pool (11 AM to 6 PM). Sit out the heat.

Day 3

Tuesday, August 4

Glacier Point Road

Gain 3,000 ft and drop 10 to 15 degrees. This is the escape day.

  • 7:30 AMLeave Curry Village. 60 mile round trip, the lot fills early.
  • 8:30 AMGlacier Point. No drinking water here, bring your own. Vault toilets only.
  • 11:00 AMSentinel Dome, 2.2 mi RT, ~400 ft, full 360 summit
  • 1:30 PMTaft Point, 2.2 mi RT, ~250 ft. Unrailed fissures, keep everyone back from the edge.
  • 4:00 PMDrive down. Tunnel View and El Capitan Meadow on the way.

For strong hikers: take the fee-based Glacier Point Tour bus one way, then hike the Four Mile Trail back down to the Valley floor, 4.8 miles all descent. Book at the tour desk.

Thursday, August 6

Hike your way home

Check out by 11:00 AM. Late check-out is charged as a full extra night.

  • 6:15 AMSunrise at Mirror Lake or Cook's Meadow

  • 10:30 AMCheck out, load the car

Shortest: Big Trees Loop only (0.3 mi, accessible), home by 7:30 PM.

Gear

Packing list

Specific to an unheated canvas tent cabin. Tick as you go.

Bear rules are not optional. No food, drinks, toiletries, or trash in the tent or the car. Everything goes in the bear locker. Fines run up to $5,000 and vehicles used for food storage can be impounded. Cooking is prohibited anywhere in Curry Village.
